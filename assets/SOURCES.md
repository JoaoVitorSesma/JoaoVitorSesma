# Where every number comes from

All README graphics are generated by [`scripts/build_assets.py`](../scripts/build_assets.py). Text is converted to outlines (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, SIL OFL 1.1, licences in `scripts/fonts/`) because GitHub does not load web fonts inside SVG images. Every animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`, leaving the final frame.

## Hero

- The hero repeats nothing from the profile sidebar: it makes one claim and draws the pipeline behind it. What I work on now and what I am exploring follow as plain text, so they stay easy to update.

## Selected work — numbers

- **37,621 patients modeled:** cohort size in the legend of the published Kaplan–Meier figure [`sources/research-km-published.png`](./sources/research-km-published.png), the image output of cell 17 in [`FOSP_curvas_Kaplan_Meier.ipynb`](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/Predicao-da-Sobrevida-em-Pacientes-com-Cancer-Colorretal-IC/blob/main/FOSP_curvas_Kaplan_Meier.ipynb).
- **55,647 records visualized:** rows in the dashboard's public CSV (see Fig. 2 below).
- **96 automated checks a day:** the monitor's Cloudflare cron runs four times an hour, as documented in its [README](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/codex-reset-monitor/blob/main/README.md).

## Fig. 1 — hazard ratios

- `exp(coef)` and 95% CI from the fitted `CoxPHFitter` summary (cell 41) in [`Predição_da_Sobrevida_em_Pacientes_com_Câncer_Colorretal_COX.ipynb`](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/Predicao-da-Sobrevida-em-Pacientes-com-Cancer-Colorretal-IC/blob/main/Predi%C3%A7%C3%A3o_da_Sobrevida_em_Pacientes_com_C%C3%A2ncer_Colorretal_COX.ipynb): 37,499 observations, 22,078 events, C-index 0.747. Data: `scripts/data/cox_hazard_ratios.json`.
- Seven of the model's covariates are shown. They are adjusted associations, not causal effects.

## Fig. 2 — diagnoses per year

- Count of records per `ANODIAG` in [`Dashboard/colo_utero1.csv`](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/Dashboard/blob/main/colo_utero1.csv), the same aggregation as the dashboard's first chart. Only yearly totals are used. Data: `scripts/data/dashboard_cases_by_year.json`.
- 2000–2023 shown; 2023 is incomplete because of registry lag, and 2024 (355 records) is omitted.
- The filter panel mirrors the controls in `DFOSP.py`.
