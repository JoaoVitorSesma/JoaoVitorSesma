# Where every number comes from

All README graphics are generated by [`scripts/build_assets.py`](../scripts/build_assets.py). Text is converted to outlines (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, SIL OFL 1.1, licences in `scripts/fonts/`) because GitHub does not load web fonts inside SVG images. Every animation stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`, leaving the final frame.

## Hero

- The hero repeats nothing from the profile sidebar: it makes one claim and draws the pipeline behind it. What I work on now and what I am exploring follow as plain text, so they stay easy to update; only their coloured labels (`assets/status/`) are images, because GitHub Markdown cannot colour text.
- The three pipeline icons are my own artwork, one render per icon and theme (`scripts/icons/source-<icon>-<light|dark>.png`). [`scripts/cut_icons.py`](../scripts/cut_icons.py) crops each one above its label, turns its background into transparency and the hero embeds it as PNG.

## Selected work — numbers

Each number previews the card below it, in the same order, and matches that card's own figures.

- **37,499 patients modeled:** observations in the fitted Cox model (see Fig. 1 below), the same count the research card shows. The wider Kaplan–Meier cohort has 37,621 patients (legend of the published figure [`sources/research-km-published.png`](./sources/research-km-published.png), the image output of cell 17 in [`FOSP_curvas_Kaplan_Meier.ipynb`](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/Predicao-da-Sobrevida-em-Pacientes-com-Cancer-Colorretal-IC/blob/main/FOSP_curvas_Kaplan_Meier.ipynb)); the README uses the model's count so the strip and the card never disagree.
- **55,647 records visualized:** rows in the dashboard's public CSV (see Fig. 2 below).
- **96 automated checks a day:** the monitor's Cloudflare cron runs four times an hour, as documented in its [README](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/codex-reset-monitor/blob/main/README.md).

## Fig. 1 — hazard ratios

- `exp(coef)` and 95% CI from the fitted `CoxPHFitter` summary (cell 41) in [`Predição_da_Sobrevida_em_Pacientes_com_Câncer_Colorretal_COX.ipynb`](https://github.com/JoaoVitorSesma/Predicao-da-Sobrevida-em-Pacientes-com-Cancer-Colorretal-IC/blob/main/Predi%C3%A7%C3%A3o_da_Sobrevida_em_Pacientes_com_C%C3%A2ncer_Colorretal_COX.ipynb): 37,499 observations, 22,078 events, C-index 0.747. Data: `scripts/data/cox_hazard_ratios.json`.
- Seven of the model's covariates are shown. They are adjusted associations, not causal effects.

## Walking strip

- The hedgehog was drawn by João and converted onto a single pixel grid and palette in `scripts/hedgehog.py` (four walking poses, crouch, curl and ball). A plain walking trip takes 20 seconds on desktop and 14 seconds on mobile. The four poses complete a cycle in approximately 440 ms, adjusted slightly to stay aligned with the full animation loop. Squares light up when planted paws touch them and fade after contact. The edges are invisible: at each one the hedgehog bumps and recovers on one visit and simply turns round on the next, and a roll that ends quietly slows down before the ball comes to rest, then pauses to catch its breath. Once per loop it also stops halfway across to glance back over its shoulder. Exposed spine borders are lighter in the dark theme. The squares under it are decorative, not real contribution data.
